UI / UX Design

Mydayss
Emotional Search
Experience

A digital self-reflection platform where users search by how they feel — and receive guided journaling prompts, affirmations, and self-care suggestions.

Role

UX/UI Designer · Brand Designer · Product Thinker

Tools

Figma · Illustrator · Notion

Duration

2–3 Weeks

The Problem

People know how they feel.
They don't know what to do with it.

Many people struggle to identify and process their emotions, making journaling feel overwhelming and inconsistent. Existing tools focus on productivity, not emotional clarity — leaving users without gentle guidance when they need it most.

The Solution

An emotional search engine — not a task manager.

Mydayss lets users type or tap how they're feeling and instantly surfaces guided journaling prompts, soft affirmations, and small self-care actions designed to meet them exactly where they are.

Emotion-first UX Gentle guidance No overwhelm Daily ritual

Learnings

What I took away

Designing for emotional states requires slowing down. Every interaction — a button label, a prompt's tone, a color — needed to feel like a gentle hand, not a push. The biggest lesson: when designing for vulnerability, restraint is a form of care.

✦

Emotional UX is about tone

Copy and microcopy mattered as much as layout.

✦

Less choices = more comfort

Reducing options in the emotion selector reduced user anxiety.

✦

Rituals beat features

Users responded to the daily ritual framing far more than app features.
