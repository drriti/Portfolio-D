Brand identity · Assignment

Riyasat

Hospitality, heritage, sustainable tourism. A full identity for a brand that revives forgotten landmarks as luxurious boutique stays, led by a promise: Leader with Heart.

01 — The name

A kingdom, an estate

Riyasat translates to "kingdom" or "estate", with a sense of grandeur and timeless tradition. The name honours artisans, their stories and the legacy of opulence.

The brand sits where heritage meets luxury. Vision, mission and values come first, because every later choice in the system answers to them.

  1. Vision. A global leader in heritage hospitality, reviving forgotten landmarks and fostering cultural preservation.
  2. Mission. Transform heritage properties into luxurious boutique stays that preserve culture and promote sustainable tourism.
  3. Values. Sustainability first: eco-friendly materials and responsible design practices.

02 — The idea

Leader with heart

The core of Riyasat is cultural revival through responsible design: pride in legacy, grounded in timeless values, courage for tomorrow.

03 — Tone of voice

Rooted, regal, empathetic, visionary

Four personalities under two principles, Heritage and Soul. For each: how we speak, how we do not, phrases we love and phrases we avoid.

04 — The logo

Custom letters, an arch for the R

The logotype is a custom typographic signature. Its curves and shapes draw on the detailing of heritage craftsmanship, and the arch of the R carries the architecture of the stays.

05 — Rules

What to do, and what never to

A brand holds together when the rules are explicit. The guidelines include misuse examples and co-branding with partners.

06 — Colour

Heritage green, ivory, golden opulence

A deep green for sophistication and tradition, ivory as a neutral canvas, gold as the accent that evokes luxury. Maroon, blush and black support.

07 — Typography

A monospace voice, a humane sans

Cascadia Code gives headlines a precise, structured identity. Source Sans Variable carries long text with warmth and legibility.

DisplayCascadia CodeHeadlines, short impactful phrases, technical content. Left-aligned, never justified.

08 — Photography

Real moments, warm light

Photography tells the stories of heritage, craft and restored spaces, with natural lighting and warm tones that celebrate the legacy being preserved.

09 — Applications

On the road, the door, the card, the page

The identity carried into a billboard, a door hanger, a business card built on a jali-arch pattern, a trifold brochure and the website.

That was Riyasat
